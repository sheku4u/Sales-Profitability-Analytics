# Sales & Profitability Analytics --- Business Problem

## 1. Project Context

This project simulates the analytics environment of a mid-sized
retail/e-commerce company selling products through multiple sales
channels and geographic regions.

The company has experienced revenue growth, but management is concerned
that profitability is not improving at the same rate. Leadership wants
to understand what is driving revenue, profit, margin changes, customer
value, product performance, regional performance, discounts, and
returns.

The analyst is expected to work with raw operational data that may
contain missing values, duplicates, inconsistent categories, invalid
records, and other data-quality problems.

The goal is not only to produce reports, but to identify business
drivers and recommend actions.

## 2. Core Business Problem

> Revenue is growing, but management does not have a clear understanding
> of why profitability varies across products, customers, regions,
> channels, and time periods.

Management needs answers to three broad questions:

1.  Where is the company generating revenue?
2.  Where is the company generating or losing profit?
3.  What business actions could improve profitable growth?

## 3. Primary Analytical Scenario

The central case study for this project is:

> **Revenue increased, but profit margin declined. What happened, why
> did it happen, and what should management do?**

The analysis should investigate possible drivers including:

-   Discounting
-   Product mix
-   Cost of goods sold
-   Sales volume
-   Customer mix
-   Regional mix
-   Sales channel mix
-   Returns
-   Changes over time

## 4. Business Objectives

### Objective 1 --- Understand revenue performance

Measure and explain:

-   Total revenue
-   Revenue growth
-   Revenue by month
-   Revenue by product
-   Revenue by category
-   Revenue by region
-   Revenue by sales channel

### Objective 2 --- Understand profitability

Measure and explain:

-   Gross profit
-   Profit margin
-   Profit by product
-   Profit by category
-   Profit by region
-   Profit by channel
-   Margin changes over time

### Objective 3 --- Identify profitability drivers

Determine whether profitability is influenced by:

-   Discount percentage
-   Product mix
-   Customer mix
-   Regional performance
-   Channel performance
-   Returns
-   Unit economics

### Objective 4 --- Understand customers

Analyze:

-   Customer contribution to revenue
-   Customer contribution to profit
-   Repeat purchasing
-   Customer segments
-   Average order value
-   Revenue concentration

### Objective 5 --- Identify business risks and opportunities

Find:

-   High-revenue/low-profit products
-   Negative-margin products
-   High-return products
-   Underperforming regions
-   Discount-heavy sales
-   Revenue concentration
-   Customers or segments with weak profitability

## 5. Expected Business Outcome

The final analysis should produce evidence-backed recommendations such
as:

-   Which products should receive more promotional attention?
-   Which products require pricing or discount review?
-   Which regions require investigation?
-   Which customer segments should be prioritized?
-   Which return drivers should be addressed?
-   Where can the company improve profitable growth?

## 6. Analytical Principle

This project follows the principle:

> **Measure → Investigate → Explain → Recommend**

A dashboard is the final communication layer, not the starting point.
