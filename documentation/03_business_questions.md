# Sales & Profitability Analytics --- Business Questions

## A. Executive Questions

1.  What is total revenue?
2.  What is total gross profit?
3.  What is the overall profit margin?
4.  Is revenue growing or declining?
5.  Is profit growing at the same rate as revenue?
6.  Which periods experienced unusual changes in revenue or profit?
7.  Are we achieving our sales and profit targets?

## B. Revenue Questions

8.  Which products generate the most revenue?
9.  Which categories generate the most revenue?
10. Which regions generate the most revenue?
11. Which sales channels generate the most revenue?
12. Which months have the highest and lowest revenue?
13. What is month-over-month revenue growth?
14. What is year-over-year revenue growth?
15. What percentage of revenue comes from the top products?
16. How concentrated is revenue among customers?

## C. Profitability Questions

17. Which products generate the most profit?
18. Which products have the highest profit margins?
19. Which products generate high revenue but low profit?
20. Which products have negative profit?
21. Which categories have the strongest margins?
22. Which categories have declining margins?
23. Which regions are most profitable?
24. Which regions generate high revenue but weak profit?
25. Which channels have the strongest margins?
26. Does higher sales volume always produce higher profit?

## D. Discount Questions

27. How does discount percentage affect profit margin?
28. Which products receive the largest discounts?
29. Which categories are most discount-dependent?
30. At what discount level does margin deteriorate?
31. Are high-discount transactions generating enough incremental revenue
    to justify the lost margin?
32. Are certain regions or channels using discounts more aggressively?

## E. Customer Questions

33. Who are the top customers by revenue?
34. Who are the top customers by profit?
35. Which customers have high revenue but low profitability?
36. What percentage of revenue comes from the top 10% of customers?
37. How many customers are repeat customers?
38. Which customer segments have the highest AOV?
39. Which customer segments have the highest profit margin?
40. Which segments have the highest return rate?

## F. Regional Questions

41. Which region is underperforming against its sales target?
42. Which region is underperforming against its profit target?
43. Why is a high-revenue region producing weak profit?
44. Which region has the highest AOV?
45. Which region has the highest return rate?
46. Are certain products successful only in specific regions?

## G. Product Questions

47. Which products should receive more promotional attention?
48. Which products should be reviewed for pricing?
49. Which products have high sales but poor margins?
50. Which products have low sales but strong margins?
51. Which products have unusually high return rates?
52. Which products contribute most to the decline in overall margin?

## H. Return Questions

53. What is the overall return rate?
54. Which products have the highest return rate?
55. Which categories have the highest return rate?
56. What are the most common return reasons?
57. Which regions have unusually high returns?
58. Are high-return products also low-profit products?
59. How much revenue or profit is associated with returned products?

## I. Advanced Case Questions

60. Revenue increased by 20%, but profit increased by only 3%. Why?
61. Revenue increased while profit decreased. What changed?
62. Which three factors explain most of the profitability decline?
63. Which products should management consider discontinuing?
64. Which products should management promote more aggressively?
65. Where is the company losing money?
66. Which customers create the greatest concentration risk?
67. If discounts were reduced by 5%, which products or categories would
    be most affected?
68. What business actions could improve profit without significantly
    reducing revenue?
69. What is the difference between revenue growth and profitable growth?
70. What are the three most important recommendations management should
    act on?

## Priority Questions for the Main Case Study

The first analysis cycle should prioritize these:

1.  Is the revenue-growth/profitability problem real?
2.  When did profitability begin to deteriorate?
3.  Which products caused the largest margin change?
4.  Did discounting contribute to the decline?
5.  Did product mix change?
6.  Did region or channel mix change?
7.  Did returns contribute materially?
8.  Which customers or segments affected the result?
9.  What are the largest drivers of profit?
10. What should management do next?
